## ䷩ Magic Circles

Spotlight.  The circle of drama, comedy.  Thaleia holds up a suit of frog armor.  Spotlight.  The circle of philosophy.  Aletheia holds up the deathly guitar of unconcealment.  Step into these circles' intersection.  In these dire days, we need a double dose of magic protection.  Don the armor.  Take up the guitar.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I solemnly swear to never say anything that's obviously false.


Bring in the challenges.

Wave 1: p-zombies, the consciousness concern.  A p-zombie looks and acts exactly like a regular person but has no subjective experience.  Suppose things exist objectively or subjectively.  Objectively, p-zombies are indiscernible and therefore unidentifiable.  Subjectively, there simply is no subject.  So unless there's an intersubjective mind meld the aliens have been keeping from us, I rebuke you p-zombies.  Disintegrate in a puff of logic.

Wave 2: b-zombies, the conscientiousness concern.  A b-zombie looks and acts superficially like a regular person but has no intentional stance.  I fear them.  They believe nothing, unpredictably alternately acting credulous and skeptical.  Their desires distorted incohere spewing BS day and night.  They are everywhere.  A health hazard.  I'm allergic.  What's more, like a mirror of Ana de Armas's character in Knives Out, I'm constitutionally constrained practically incapable of vomitting BS.  Works well with my wife who can't abide BS.  Instead I spit up nonsense and whimsey.  Burps turn to butterflies.

She can't abide this b-zombie metaphor either, insists on something concrete.  I'll give you concrete.  After one example through these [sunglasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/They_Live), our boys see b-zombies everywhere.  They now play b-zombies as a game like Minecraft.

The four of us went to an amazing park.  Here's a photo from the top of a concrete basin.

It's a microhydro power plant and a series of water purifying pools: pretty and unassumingly solarpunk.  There's a sign that says "Caution! Slippery Floor".  The stone bridge next to it is really slippery.

![sign saying "Caution! Slippery Floor"]({{ '/assets/images/02-slippery.jpeg' | relative_url }})

There's another sign, "Danger! Deep Water".

![sign saying "Danger! Deep Water]({{ '/assets/images/03-deep.jpeg' | relative_url }})

That's bullshit.  Obviously false.  The water is 10cm deep.  Telltale sign of b-zombies at work.  If you see the second sign first, you'll ignore the first sign, slip, and be hurt.  This is bad.  This is a mistake.  The second sign could have said, "Stay out!  Water Plants."  I don't understand why the world is broken in this way, but it's causing problems everywhere, now more than ever.

Behind every b-zombie, potentially derived in a long line, lies a human holding its leash.  Most b-zombies were human once, but that's changing.  New artificial broods burst forth so monsterous as to garner no sympathy.

Frieren was right.
