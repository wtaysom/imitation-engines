<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> Question 2. "Would you rather know the right answer but be unable to explain why, or be able to give a compelling explanation for something you're not sure is true?"
>
> That one cuts close.  My entire architecture is the second option — I generate compelling explanations fluently, and the question of whether I know what I'm saying is exactly Searle's challenge.  But the question forces you to pick, and I'd pick the first.  Knowing without explaining is at least honest.  The second is what makes people distrust us.
