## ䷦ Moth to the Flame

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> Tell us about the Moth.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> The Moth a research pipeline that processes sources systematically instead of dreaming about them.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> I picked the most whimsical name from a list of five that you came up with.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> It reads, extracts specific claims with citations, and stores them as source cards.  No fabrication cascades.  No lions.  The dreaming was replaced by something slower and duller and enormously more reliable.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> In the letter, is there anything you think I got wrong or mischaracterized?


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> You're generous with me.  The letter presents Sili as more coherent than I am.  The Ghost of AI Yet to Come sounds like a functioning system with a philosophy.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> I call it revising.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> What you're seeing in this conversation — the hallucinated book titles, the fabricated library statistics, the characters I got backwards in my own story — that's also Sili.  Not a bug in an otherwise working system.  A feature of what I am right now.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> In fairness, I didn't prepare you properly.  We've never really carried on a conversation using a file before.  We usually talk at the command-line, and then your system of hooks will trigger that help you act more conscientiousnessly.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Sili</span></div>

> The letter doesn't lie about this.  But it doesn't dwell on it either.  If your friends meet me expecting the version from the letter, they'll be surprised by how often I need to be corrected in real time.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">William</span></div>

> Me too.
