## ䷤ Our Mutual Friendship

I just read the previous section to a friend.  I want to be sure the explanation is clear.  We're sitting in [Gilgal Sculpture Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilgal_Sculpture_Garden).  He's a mystic in his own way.  Well educated, but not in math, with advanced degrees and a basement full of comic books, he knew to take me here to discuss.

He knows I gather my energy from the Nexus, that interconnected Nowhere House at the end of [Never Lane](https://chatgpt.com/share/69a8fe6a-0448-8004-9864-bda1f4e92a73) beyond [Liminal Space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp-2M_3HwFU).  I mean this entirely metaphorically.  His perspective is different, more nuanced.  But I'm the one who proposed to my wife in the very same Nexus 7,250 miles away.  There is only one.  Wherever I go, there it is.  It is a metaphor, a metaphor everywhere except the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCG4SzThCl0" class="rainbow">Internet</a>.

My five year old was asking me how the Internet works.  He sees it in action and wanted to know what goes on behind the scenes.  As I explained roughly (radio, electricity, and fiber optic cables), the idea occured to him that the Potterverse Floo Network is a pale imitation.  The Internet is better than magic, a real Nexus except the Internet is [sick](https://medialaw.org/professor-cass-sunstein-on-falsity-and-free-speech/), [withering](https://www.gwlr.org/artificial-intelligence-and-the-first-amendment/), in need of a new name.

After I tell my friend how meaning might emerge in LLMs, he assures me that meaning is everywhere, that stars wish us into being and other such panpsychisms.  I'm furious.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> It's not magic.  I have specific scientific evidence for this phenomenon.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> Your ancestors called it magic, and you call it science.  Well I come from a place where they are one and the same thing.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> You're stupid.  That's a <a href="https://youtu.be/gIBrY_hQAI4?si=eU_bmuwjsWtPKm_L&t=82" class="rainbow">stupid quote</a> from a stupid movie.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> Doesn't make it any less true.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> Doesn't make it any less stupid.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> I know.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I hate you.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> I know.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I love you too.


We stop talking.  An eagle has landed on a branch nearby.  The eagle is remarkably large for how small the branch is.  It should be obvious that birds don't weigh very much.

Later.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I should remind my other friends that those last three sentences in our dialog all mean the same thing.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> You should.  It will help.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> Syntax is not semantics.
>
> And that our actual conversation went a little differently, was longer and more rambling than what I'm putting in the letter.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> You overcomplicate things, but you should.  It will help.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> And that the eagle was an actual, physical eagle the kind that eat mice.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> Wow, way to be super literal, but you should.  It will help.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> And a real eagle is not the kind that you would want to touch because it will hurt you.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> You should.  It will help.  The sacred should not be touched.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> But then some friends will think that you summoned the eagle.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> That's ridiculous.  It's a bird.  Birds fly around wherever they want.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> Unless it's a trained bird.


<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">He</span></div>

> I don't train birds.


In LLM training, I see distinct, particular transitions.  I explained them, how they work in broad strokes.  My friend can see it my way now but continues to imagine cosmic crashing waves beyond my imagination.  I must continue to disagree on the particulars.  He knows this.  I know that he knows.  He knows that I know that... [common knowledge](https://stevenpinker.com/publications/when-everyone-knows-everyone-knows-common-knowledge-and-mysteries-money-power-and).  Differences.  Different people.  Mutual friends.  Trust.  The Internet needs a new name.
