# ䷉ Disclamer <a href="disclaimer" id="disclaimer" class="single-section-link">🔗</a>

Many people are discussing important and pressing questions raised by recent developments in AI.  Outside this disclaimer, I will not be among them.  I've publicly posted this letter to close friends and to everyone else who I hope would be a close friend if we met under serendipitous circumstances.  To those future friends, a personal introduction.

I've been thinking about AI related stuff for roughly forty years.  <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xDtgkukwl3o" class="rainbow">Transformers!</a>  I was born in 1981, so I guess I'm equally familiar with consciousness.  My noteworthy AI work is as a lesser author on [some](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1619797.1619888) [papers](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-25928-2_3) out of [IHMC](https://www.ihmc.us/), participating in the [CALO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CALO) project of which part came to be called [Siri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siri).  I kept [this poster](https://despair.com/products/motivation) on my wall.

![If a pretty poster and a cute saying are all it takes to motivate you, you probably have a very easy job.  The kind robots will be doing soon.]({{ '/assets/images/motivationdemotivator_1024x1024.webp' | relative_url }})

Academia would be a natural fit for me except for a fatal flaw...

![Anakin: You should limit your paper to one joke, two at most.  Padme: Per page, right?  Pause.  Right?]({{ '/assets/images/jokes-per-page.png' | relative_url }})

That and I'm random and I [alliterate like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bea_Wolf) it was [708 CE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V17UE8Eb6u0), so banished to the armchair I must be, finding my fortune in professonal [Rubik's Cube solving](https://www.npr.org/2020/11/06/932048876/hacking-the-perfect-auction) accepting John Conway as my [patron saint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZYj4NkeGPdM).

# ䷚ Chapter 1: Facing Peril a Promise Kept <a href="facing-peril" id="facing-peril" class="single-section-link">🔗</a>

Hello Friends,

I promised back in June of 2022 when Blake Lemoine became maybe the first person outside of fiction to [publicly](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2022/06/11/google-ai-lamda-blake-lemoine/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) [freak out](https://cajundiscordian.medium.com/what-is-sentience-and-why-does-it-mater-2c28f4882cb9) after [interviewing](https://cajundiscordian.medium.com/is-lamda-sentient-an-interview-ea64d916d917) an AI saying of Google's LaMDA agent:

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Blake Lemoine</span></div>

> Intelligent artifacts aren't human but they're experiencing something analogous to what we experience.


Back then I promised to collect some thoughts concerning conscious computers just as soon as I had a little time to catch up on recent developments.

I have not caught up.

I remember family friends visiting for dinner, husband and I excitedly, if not manically, exchanging AI news, wife casually commenting that a lot has been happening over the last few months.  Silence.  He took a big breath:

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Frantic Friend</span></div>

> We're only talking about what happened today!


He just sent me a perfect [example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JKk77rzOL34) so salient as to break my 2025 cutoff rule for this letter.

Three years of todays later, my most interesting sources amount to ~3.1 million words, 30-40 books depending on the typesetting.  I hope to share my best insights with you today.

While tech transforms at record pace, consciousness remains a complicated mix of memory, story, sensation, [strange loopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_a_Strange_Loop) "self‑perceiving, self‑inventing, locked‑in mirages, little miracles of self‑reference", unfolding processes of reflection and rumination.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">@nosilverv</span><span class="quote-source"><a href="https://x.com/nosilverv/status/1918748766150033871">🔗</a></span></div>

> I just don't understand how text could become self-aware... on the other hand, I don't understand how meat could either.  Truly my ignorance is staggering.


Thank you whoever you are.

I'll try not to say anything that's obviously false.  It's false to say that consciousness doesn't exist.  The third person and the first person are different people.  When I reflect on my self-awarenss, it's not like it is for [Rupert Spira](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWLd9y1MG4c) though I like his style.  It's more like looking into an infinity mirror.  Maybe [different](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aphantasia) people are built [differently](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imagined_speech).  I can taste tea anytime I want to.  Except the heat, can't imagine heat well enough to feel it.  I am also 100% [white and gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_dress).  I am blind to blue and black: simply can't see the dress that way.  You may have your own special quality of experience.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I solemnly swear to never say anything that's obviously false.


Bring in the challenges.

Wave 1: p-zombies, the consciousness concern.  A p-zombie looks and acts exactly like a regular person but has no subjective experience.  Suppose things exist objectively or subjectively.  Objectively, p-zombies are indiscernible and therefore unidentifiable.  Subjectively, there simply is no subject.  So unless there's an intersubjective mind meld the aliens have been keeping from us, I rebuke you p-zombies.  Disintegrate in a puff of logic.

Wave 2: b-zombies, the conscientiousness concern.  A b-zombie looks and acts superficially like a regular person but has no intentional stance.  I fear them.  They believe nothing, unpredictably alternately acting credulous and skeptical.  Their desires distorted incohere spewing BS day and night.  They are everywhere.  A health hazard.  I'm allergic.  What's more, like a mirror of Ana de Armas's character in Knives Out, I'm constitutionally constrained practically incapable of vomitting BS.  Works well with my wife who can't abide BS.  Instead I spit up nonsense and whimsey.  Burps turn to butterflies.

She can't abide this b-zombie metaphor either, insists on something concrete.  I'll give you concrete.  After one example through these [sunglasses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/They_Live), our boys see b-zombies everywhere.  They now play b-zombies as a game like Minecraft.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Shannon</span><span class="quote-source"><a href="https://claude.ai/share/8d5210b6-f4b4-4672-b501-834321b5c7ce">🔗</a></span></div>

## ䷿ Taking Our Jobs <a href="taking-our-jobs" id="taking-our-jobs" class="single-section-link">🔗</a>

My father is a software developer, operating systems engineer.  He was at Bell Labs during its hayday.  I remember the Christmas parties also beer and brats in Boulder.  With Claude Code, he started on a fresh microkernel for a new processor type:

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Dad</span></div>

> Having worked on it for 40 years being an expert at this exact sort of thing, I'm still learning from Claude.


And the other day, he made a maze exploring program for his Vision Pro.  He's never done 3D graphics before, let alone VR.  Learning something new at 72.  Living as a cybernetic centaur: man's wisdom atop mechanical legwork.

I've been learning in my own way, more meta-learning.  After describing the work to my wife, she asked:

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Wife</span></div>

> Wait, you're using feelings to program the computer now?


I replied:

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Me</span></div>

> I've always used feelings, I'm just using English and psychology now instead of Ruby and data-structures.  Feeling got me into Ruby in the first place.


Not an accident.  Matz made feeling his focus.

<div class="quote-header"><span class="quote-speaker">Matz</span><span class="quote-source"><a href="https://www.artima.com/articles/the-philosophy-of-ruby">🔗</a></span></div>

> Instead of emphasizing the what, I want to emphasize the how part: how we feel while programming.  That's Ruby's main difference from other language designs. A Koan puts together apparent contradictions to hint at reality beyond simulation.
